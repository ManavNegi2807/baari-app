      Priority membership for patients

      Patients who pay you (cash or UPI at the desk) become priority members for a set number of days. You mark them as paid in the Appointments tab, and Baari gives them the perks below. Emergencies are always seen first, whatever the plan.

      The first times of each day are kept for members.
      So unused times are not wasted.
      Everyone else books up to 7 days ahead.

      Members also move up to 30 minutes ahead in the live queue and are offered cancelled times first.
